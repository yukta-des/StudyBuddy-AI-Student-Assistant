# StudyBuddy — AI Student Assistant

An AI-powered academic study assistant designed to guide students through coding, database, and logical problems with progressive hint tracking, step-by-step solutions, and a gamified achievement case.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **5 Progressive Socratic Hints**:
  - The AI analyzes the problem difficulty (1–3) and subject.
  - Provides hints that guide the student closer to the answer step-by-step.
  - **Popup solution triggers**: Requesting the 4th (second-to-last) or 5th (last) hint displays a popup modal offering to show the full worked solution.

- **Keys & Hints Stats Dashboard**:
  - Displays visual status bars showing how many questions you solved using 5 hints.
  - Tracks individual solve counts for each category Key.

- **Achievements Case** (Unlocks after solving **3 questions**):
  - 🥉 **Bronze**:Solve 5 questions with difficulty level 3 (Default starting stage once unlocked).
  - 🥈 **Silver**: Solve 15 questions + use ≤ 2 hints on at least 3 of them with diff level 3.
  - 🥇 **Gold**: Solve 25 questions + solve at least 5 questions with 1 hint with diff level 3.
  - 💎 **Platinum**: Solve 30 questions + solve at least 7questions with 0 hints with diff level 3.
  - 👑 **Master**: Solve 50 questions + solve at least 10 questions with 0 hints with difficulty level 3.
